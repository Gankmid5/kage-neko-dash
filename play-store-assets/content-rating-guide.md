# Google Play Store Submission & Content Rating Guide — Kage-Neko Dash

## 1. Content Rating (IARC Questionnaire)
- **Category:** Game
- **Violence:** Mild / Stylized Fantasy Violence (cartoony/silhouette ninja cat slashes stylized enemy gunners and deflects projectiles; no blood, gore, or realistic human violence).
- **Sexual Content / Nudity:** No
- **Profanity / Crude Humor:** No
- **Drugs / Alcohol / Tobacco:** No
- **Gambling / Simulated Gambling:** No
- **User-Generated Content / Chat / Social:** No
- **Shares User Location:** No
- **In-App Purchases / Ads:** No (if you release it free with no ads/IAP)

*Expected Rating:* **ESRB Everyone / Everyone 10+ (E / E10+)** and **PEGI 7**.

---

## 2. Data Safety Form (Google Play Console)
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** → **No**
- **Is all user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** → N/A (no data collected or transmitted)
- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** → N/A

---

## 3. App Access & Target Audience
- **App Access:** All functionality is available without special access (no login required).
- **Ads:** No, my app does not contain ads.
- **Target Age Group:** 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+ (or 13+ if you prefer not to opt into the Families Policy review).

---

## 4. Privacy Policy URL
Host `privacy-policy.html` (for example on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your personal site) and paste the HTTPS URL into **App content → Privacy policy** in Google Play Console.
