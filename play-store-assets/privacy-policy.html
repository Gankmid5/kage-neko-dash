Privacy Policy — Kage-Neko Dash

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Kage-Neko Dash is an offline-capable arcade parkour game. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your experience simple and safe.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage analytics.

  • No accounts, logins, or registration are required.
  • No advertising SDKs, third-party trackers, or analytics tools are included.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device identifiers are accessed.

2. Local Storage

The game saves your unlocked stage progress locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy

Because Kage-Neko Dash does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

4. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional online features (such as leaderboards), this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play store page.
